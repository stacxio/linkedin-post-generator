# Industry Trend Analysis Template

## Structure

```
[HOOK — name the trend and take a clear position on it]

[What's happening — describe the trend briefly with evidence]

[What most people think — the conventional take]

[What's actually going on — your sharper, more informed perspective]

[What this means for [audience] — practical implications]

[Your prediction or recommendation — where this is heading]

[Discussion prompt]

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3
```

## Example

```
AI agents are the most over-hyped and under-built category in tech right now.

Every SaaS company is slapping "AI agent" on their landing page. VC decks are full of "autonomous workflows." The hype cycle is in full swing.

The common take: "Agents will replace most knowledge work within 2 years."

What's actually happening: Most "AI agents" are glorified if/else chains with an LLM call in the middle. They work for demos. They break in production. The gap between "impressive prototype" and "reliable system" is massive.

What this means for builders: The opportunity isn't in building another agent framework. It's in building the reliability layer — the error handling, the human-in-the-loop checkpoints, the monitoring that makes AI workflows actually trustworthy.

This is exactly where tools like n8n shine. You can orchestrate AI calls within a visual workflow, add fallbacks, monitor every step, and keep a human in the loop without writing a custom orchestration layer from scratch.

My prediction: The winners in the AI agent space won't be the ones with the most autonomous systems. They'll be the ones whose systems fail gracefully.

What's your take — are AI agents overhyped or are we just early?

#AITrends #AIEngineering #BuildWithAI #stacx24
```

## Tips
- Take a clear position — "it depends" is not a trend analysis
- Reference specific examples or data when possible
- The "what most people think vs. what's actually happening" contrast is the core engine
- Connect the trend back to stacx24's domain naturally
- Predictions make posts more engaging and shareable
