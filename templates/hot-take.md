# Hot Take Template

## Structure

```
[BOLD OPENING STATEMENT — contrarian, surprising, or challenges a common belief]

[1-2 sentences of context — why most people get this wrong]

[The evidence — concrete example, data point, or personal experience that backs the claim]

[The nuance — acknowledge the other side briefly, then reinforce your position]

[Landing — what the reader should actually do differently]

[Question to drive comments]

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3
```

## Example

```
Most "automation" is just moving manual work to a different screen.

I see teams spending weeks setting up Zapier workflows that break every time an API changes. They call it automation. I call it a more complicated way to do the same thing manually.

Real automation means self-healing workflows. Error handling built in. Retry logic that doesn't need a human babysitter.

We moved 12 client workflows from Zapier to self-hosted n8n. Result: 94% fewer intervention-needed alerts in 30 days.

Yes, there's a learning curve. No, it's not as steep as rebuilding from scratch every quarter.

What's the automation in your stack that still needs you to babysit it?

#n8nAutomation #WorkflowAutomation #stacx24
```

## Tips
- The bolder the opening, the more you need evidence to back it up
- Don't be contrarian just for engagement — have a real point
- The nuance paragraph prevents you from sounding like a troll
- End question should be easy to answer (low barrier to comment)
