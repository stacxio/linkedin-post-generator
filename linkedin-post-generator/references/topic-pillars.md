# stacx24 Topic Pillars

Three content pillars form the foundation of stacx24's LinkedIn authority. Each pillar has sub-topics, recurring angles, and content hooks.

---

## Pillar 1: n8n Automation

### What it covers
Everything about n8n workflow automation — from beginner-friendly introductions to advanced self-hosted enterprise patterns. stacx24 positions itself as the go-to resource for people who want to automate without writing boilerplate code (or who want to extend n8n with custom code when needed).

### Sub-topics
- **Workflow design patterns**: Error handling, retry logic, conditional branching, parallel execution
- **Integration recipes**: Connecting APIs, databases, SaaS tools (Slack, Notion, Airtable, Stripe, etc.)
- **Self-hosting**: Running n8n on your own infrastructure, Docker/Kubernetes setups, security considerations
- **n8n vs. alternatives**: When to choose n8n over Zapier, Make, or custom scripts (and when not to)
- **Real-world automations**: Client stories, internal workflow improvements, time-saved metrics
- **Advanced patterns**: Webhook orchestration, sub-workflows, credential management, queue-based processing

### Recurring content angles
- "I automated X in Y minutes using n8n — here's the workflow"
- "Stop paying $500/mo for Zapier. Here's why n8n is the answer."
- "The n8n workflow pattern nobody talks about"
- "3 automations every [role] should have running today"
- "We replaced [manual process] with a 5-node n8n workflow"

## Pillar 2: Full Stack Development

### What it covers
Modern web development practices, architecture decisions, developer productivity, and the craft of shipping software. stacx24 speaks to both frontend and backend developers who care about building things that work well and ship fast.

### Sub-topics
- **Architecture decisions**: Monolith vs. microservices, serverless patterns, API design (REST vs. GraphQL)
- **Frontend frameworks**: React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte — when to use what and why
- **Backend patterns**: Node.js, Express, database design, caching strategies, auth flows
- **DevOps & deployment**: CI/CD, Docker, cloud platforms, monitoring, zero-downtime deploys
- **Developer productivity**: Tools, workflows, code review culture, shipping velocity
- **Career & craft**: Technical decision-making, working with non-technical stakeholders, code quality vs. speed

### Recurring content angles
- "The stack I'd choose in 2026 (and why it's not what you think)"
- "Stop over-engineering. Ship the MVP."
- "The one architecture decision that saved us 6 months"
- "Why [popular practice] is actually holding your team back"
- "Full stack doesn't mean you do everything — it means you understand everything"

## Pillar 3: AI Trends

### What it covers
Practical AI implementation, cutting through the hype, and showing how AI actually fits into real workflows and products. stacx24 is the bridge between AI hype and AI execution — focused on what developers and businesses can actually ship today.

### Sub-topics
- **LLM integrations**: Building with OpenAI, Anthropic, local models — practical patterns and pitfalls
- **AI-powered workflows**: Combining n8n with AI (document processing, classification, content generation)
- **What's real vs. what's hype**: Honest assessment of AI capabilities and limitations
- **AI tooling landscape**: Which tools are worth adopting, which are just noise
- **AI in production**: Prompt engineering patterns, evaluation frameworks, cost management, latency optimization
- **Future trends**: Agents, multi-modal AI, code generation, where things are heading

### Recurring content angles
- "I replaced [manual task] with an AI workflow. Here's what happened."
- "The AI tool nobody's talking about (that actually works)"
- "Stop building AI features nobody asked for"
- "Here's what AI can't do yet (and what to do about it)"
- "The $0 AI stack that outperforms most paid tools"

## Cross-Pillar Content

The most powerful posts often sit at the intersection of two or three pillars:

- **n8n + AI**: "I built an AI-powered content pipeline using n8n and Claude — zero code, 100% automated"
- **Full Stack + AI**: "How we added AI features to our app without blowing up our cloud bill"
- **n8n + Full Stack**: "Why every full stack developer should know n8n (it's not just for non-coders)"
- **All three**: "The modern builder's stack: n8n for automation, [framework] for the app, AI for the intelligence layer"

Cross-pillar posts tend to perform best because they demonstrate breadth of expertise and give the audience unexpected connections they can act on.
